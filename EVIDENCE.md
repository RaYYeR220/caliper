# Evidence

Every public claim this project makes, tagged by what actually backs it, with a link to the thing
you would check. The point of the exercise is the bottom section: the things we deliberately do
**not** claim.

Tiers:

- **REPRODUCIBLE** — you can rerun it here and get the same answer, with no key and no network.
- **VERIFIED** — checked against an external source, or enforced by a test in this repository.
- **MEASURED** — a number from our own run, against our own answer key.
- **MODELLED** — an estimate or an extrapolation, stated as one.
- **NOT CLAIMED** — see the last section.

| Claim | Tier | Check it |
|---|---|---|

| Compiling criteria to executable form is not novel | VERIFIED | Criteria2Query (JAMIA 2019), OHDSI ATLAS cohort expressions compiled by `circe-be` |
| Three-valued logic with null propagation is not novel | VERIFIED | the CQL logical specification; our evaluator implements the same semantics deliberately |

## Not claimed

Written out because a claims list is only as useful as the things it excludes.

- **Not a medical device.** Not validated for clinical use, not evaluated on real patients, not
  reviewed by a regulator.
- **No clinician-validated ground truth.** The answer key is model-assisted dual annotation with
  human adjudication. We are not clinicians and did not obtain clinician review.
- **No claim about enrolment.** We measured screening decisions on synthetic charts. We did not
  measure enrolment, screen-failure rate, or time saved by a real coordinator, and the minutes-per-
  pair figure in the problem statement is cited from the literature, not from our own observation.
- **No claim of statistical significance.** Roughly fifty cases. Every proportion carries an exact
  interval spanning about thirteen percentage points.
- **No claim that a live run reproduces byte for byte.** It does not, and `REPRODUCE.md` explains
  why temperature zero is not determinism on a hosted model.
- **No claim that the terminology mappings are complete or audited.** Codes are shape-checked, not
  validated against a terminology server.
- **No claim that the prose linter verifies meaning.** It verifies that every number is bound to a
  value the criterion resolved from. A sentence with every number correct can still describe the
  wrong relationship between them.
- **No claim that the critic catches every compiler error.** It catches differences visible between
  two English sentences. A criterion compiled with a plausible wrong threshold, rendered into a
  sentence that reads correctly, passes.
- **No claim about trials outside the ten in `data/trials/`,** or about disease areas the corpus does
  not cover. The oncology trial is included to show a limit honestly, not to be scored well.
- **No claim that the coverage-gated absence rule is correct.** It is a modelling assumption. The
  alternatives are implemented and reported beside it so the size of the assumption is visible.
- **No claim that the corrected answer key is right, only that it is less wrong.** The corrections
  were made after seeing a scored run, which is the circumstance in which a correction is least
  trustworthy. Both keys ship, both are scored, and the rule that bounded every change — a label
  moved only where the chart refutes it, with the refuting value quoted — is published so a reader
  can disagree line by line.
- **No claim that `ELIGIBLE` means the patient is eligible.** It means nothing in the record rules
  them out and the criteria that remain are settled at the screening visit. An exclusion settled at
  the visit can still rule them out afterwards. `LIMITS.md` states the cost precisely.
